# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Visitors are buyers choosing bath bombs as a personal ritual, a gift, or a small branded batch for partners and teams. They arrive from social links, search, referrals, or direct brand traffic and need to understand the product, trust the maker, and submit an order without messaging back and forth.

## Product Purpose

Posleslovie is a marketing and lead-generation site for handmade bath bombs and gift sets. Success means the page feels premium enough to justify a purchase, explains the offer quickly, and carries the visitor into the checkout or partner/contact modal. The checkout flow must preserve its existing AmoCRM lead submission, validation, logo upload, call scheduling, legal documents, analytics, SEO, and static export constraints.

## Brand Personality

Private, tactile, and cinematic. The brand should feel like a considered bath ritual rather than a craft-market souvenir: sensory, precise, calm, and quietly confident. Copy can be rewritten for the redesign if it makes the experience stronger.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic AI landing-page grammar: purple/blue mesh gradients, three equal feature cards, endless tiny uppercase section labels, decorative fake dashboards, noisy glassmorphism, beige-and-brass craft clichés, and template luxury that looks like every cosmetics landing page. The redesign does not need to preserve the current Figma composition or old section order.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with atmosphere, then remove friction.
2. Treat every CTA as a concierge handoff, not a generic button.
3. Use real product assets and stable local image paths.
4. Make motion feel expensive, purposeful, and interruptible.
5. Preserve business-critical flows while allowing the surface to be reinvented.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target keyboard-operable, focus-visible, responsive UI with readable contrast and touch targets suitable for mobile visitors. Honor `prefers-reduced-motion`, keep modals focus-trapped, preserve form labels/errors, and avoid effects that hide content before animation completes.
